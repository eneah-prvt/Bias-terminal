Growth and inflation data from FRED place the economy in one of four regimes: Goldilocks, Reflation, Stagflation or Deflation. A weighted macro composite from −1 to +1 translates the regime into a risk posture.

§ 3 — Methodology

How the bias is built

The Bias Score is built in three stages. Four FRED macro series form a macro score; CFTC positioning is blended in at 25%, then dealer flow from the options market at 15%. Every signal is scaled to −1 … +1 before it is weighted.

Exhibit 7: Signals and weights in the composite

Weights as displayed in the terminal. Stage 1 weights are coefficients on the four macro signals; stage 2 blends the macro score 75 / 25 with COT; stage 3 blends the result 85 / 15 with dealer flow.

Data sources

Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED) for rates, volatility, the dollar and credit; the CFTC Commitments of Traders report for positioning; index options for dealer gamma; live ES and NQ futures bars for intraday levels.

Refresh frequency

Macro inputs every few minutes during market hours, dealer gamma every 60 seconds in session, COT weekly after the Friday release.

Limitations

The read is context, not a forecast. COT data is published weekly with a lag, dealer positioning is an estimate from public options data and can shift quickly around expiries, and the weights are fixed rather than fitted to recent results.
